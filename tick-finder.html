1 · Start

Mic off. Engine running, doors closed, radio off.

Phone voice processing (echo cancel, noise suppression, auto-gain) is requested OFF so readings are comparable. Levels are relative dBFS, not calibrated SPL. Keep the phone's mic (usually the bottom edge) pointed the same way at every spot.

2 · Fingerprint the tick (fan OFF vs ON)

Hold the phone in one spot. Capture with fan off, then fan on. The bands where ON rises over OFF and the signal is impulsive and periodic are where the tick lives. Tap the spectrum to set a custom band.

4 · Record spots & fan speeds

Locations: same fan speed, same phone orientation, about a hand's width from each surface. Fan-speed steps: same spot, label each "Fan 1", "Fan 2"… so the app can test whether the tick rate follows blower speed.

Field procedure & caveats
  1. Engine idling (or key-on, engine off, to rule out engine noise), radio off, windows up, A/C compressor off if possible so the clutch cycling isn't confused for a tick.
  2. Fingerprint: capture fan OFF, then fan ON at the speed where the tick is loudest.
  3. Fan-speed sweep from one spot (passenger footwell is usually closest to the blower). Rate rising with speed points at something turning with the blower wheel.
  4. Location sweep at one fixed fan speed. Typical spots: passenger footwell / behind glovebox (blower housing on most trucks and SUVs), center stack, driver footwell, each vent outlet, defrost slots, and outside under the cowl / windshield base on the passenger side (fresh-air intake).
  5. Also try Recirc vs Fresh and Vent vs Floor vs Defrost modes. A tick that changes with mode doors points toward a door, actuator or flap in the box rather than the blower.

Limits: tick rate is detectable from about 3 to 300 per second. The phone mic and the cabin's reflections color the readings, so trust the relative ranking between spots more than any absolute number. This is a locating aid, not a diagnosis.
