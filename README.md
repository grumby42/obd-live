# OBD Live

A single-page, read-only OBD-II dashboard for an OBDLink LX (Bluetooth Classic) using Chrome's Web Serial API.

- Use Chrome 148+ on Android, or Chrome/Edge on Windows. Samsung Internet and iOS browsers aren't supported.
- Pair the adapter in your device's Bluetooth settings, turn the ignition on, then tap **Connect LX**.
- Only allowlisted read requests can reach the vehicle. It can't clear codes or write to any module.
- Data stays on your device. The page's security policy blocks all network requests after load; nothing is uploaded. Exports exclude the VIN, and exclude position unless you allow it. The camera is never used and audio is never stored.

## Features
- Separate profiles, history, limits, and layouts for each vehicle, chosen automatically from the VIN. The VIN stays on the device and isn't exported.
- Logs every standard reading a vehicle reports, plus computer self-test results. Unknown standard PIDs and GM requests found by the scan are logged as raw values.
- Saves drives about every 5 seconds on the device and offers to resume after a refresh or dropped connection.
- Optional phone sensors: GPS speed, altitude and grade, motion (vibration level and frequency), and microphone level. Off by default; position is recorded only if you turn it on.
- Saves full drives on the device and produces a drive review for city, highway, or mixed trips.

Run the tests from the repo root: `node test/scenarios.test.js`

Readings tagged **Unverified** come from community reports for other GM vehicles and must be checked against a known value. Green/red limits are starting guides unless stated otherwise. This tool doesn't replace professional diagnosis.

### Vehicles without a gear request (2006 Lucerne, 4T65-E)
- Gear is inferred from engine rpm per mph against the transmission's published ratio set: one anchor (final drive × tire) is fitted per vehicle, each steady sample is matched to its nearest band, and the fit is rejected if the bands don't match the ratio spacing. Shift detection then runs on the inferred gear and says so.
- EVAP: tank-pressure readings that never move, tank vacuum beyond GM's P0446 set point, and purge duty are reported, with an "assume guilty" thesis for a blocked vent. A gas-cap test (key on, engine off, Mark "cap", loosen the cap) separates a real vacuum from a sensor reporting a fixed value.
- Code history: miles with the light on vs miles since the last clear.

### Trip computer and fuel planning (aviation-style)
- Vehicle settings take the tank size (owner's manual) and a reserve you want to keep. From fuel level, airflow-based burn rate and a rolling 10-minute window the app shows fuel in tank, burn rate, specific range (mpg), range to empty, range to reserve, and endurance to reserve and to empty in h:mm, plus trip distance, fuel used, average mpg, engine time and moving average speed since connect.
- The review tables cruise performance by 10-mph band from steady, level samples, like an aircraft cruise chart, and names the best-economy band.
- The fuel, range and endurance numbers are available as widgets for any custom screen; the former "Trip" page is folded into Glance (below).

### Glance cockpit, Mark bar, and usual bands
- Glance is one no-scroll cockpit tile: rpm dial with the road speed and gear stacked at its centre, a row of coolant / trans fluid / oil °F / oil psi, cylinder mode (V4/V8) with the per-cylinder misfire counters (deactivated cylinders dimmed in V4), fuel remaining, miles remaining (rolling 10-minute burn against the tank size) and module voltage, and the four tire pressures (DF/DR/PF/PR = driver/passenger front/rear). It re-flows into dial-left, numbers-right in landscape. The dial's green/amber/red zones are visual only.
- Annunciators (crew-alerting style) sit at the top of the cockpit: red for oil pressure low, coolant/trans/oil hot, voltage under 12 V, and no data from the adapter for 8 s; amber for check-engine light, a misfire counter rising during the drive, a low tire, fuel at or under 15%, EVAP tank pressure beyond the P0446 set point (±1,000 Pa), converter slip at cruise, and a measure beyond anything in this vehicle's history. A red one flashes until the strip is tapped and dims the unrelated rows; after the tap the chip stays (steady) as long as the condition does and the rows return to normal; amber chips retire from the strip after 15 s (the reading itself stays rated); a ⚑ counter at the start of the strip opens the alert history for the drive in trigger order (time, text, speed, rpm, level), which is saved with the drive and exported; each new one chimes (three beeps red, two tones amber; off in Phone sensors) and, with "Speak" on, is spoken. Cockpit numbers go amber within the approach margin of a limit (5°F coolant/trans, 10°F oil, 5 psi oil pressure, 0.3 V), red past it; fuel amber at 30%, red at 15%. Oil temperature (250°F), oil pressure (10 psi running) and EVAP (±1,000 Pa) now carry starting-guide rules.
- The rpm dial has ticks every 250 rpm (long at each 1,000), scale numbers inside the band, and a triangle pointer whose tip rides the inside of the band and eases between samples instead of stepping.
- Each annunciator is a button: check-engine opens Diagnostics, a metric opens its drill-in (or its screen while moving), no-data opens the connection log. The check-engine light that was already on at connect stays amber; if the code count rises during the drive it goes red, flashes, chimes, re-reads the codes and names the new one.
- The bottom bar is fixed on every screen: Connect/Disconnect on the left, MARK in the middle (enabled while connected; vibrates on tap), full-screen toggle on the right (hides the browser and system bars where the browser allows; otherwise add the page to the home screen). While moving it pins the mark at once; stopped, it opens the note dialog (cap tests etc.).
- Usual bands: for each measure (shift quality, slip, shift times, fluid/coolant/oil peaks, pressures, voltage, trims, misfires, knock, mpg, EVAP, catalyst, tires) the app keeps the 10th–90th percentile across this vehicle's own drives of 5+ minutes (5 drives minimum). Once a repair is logged in Vehicle settings and 5+ drives precede it, the bands freeze at the pre-repair drives, so drives after the repair are judged against the vehicle as it was and never widen the band. Live: a "Vs. usual bands" tile (Health check, Transmission) and amber cockpit numbers list measures outside their band after 5 minutes (averages), 10 minutes (peaks) or 5 upshifts (shift quality); "new high/low" means beyond anything logged. Every drive review adds a "Compared with this vehicle's history" card. Drives saved by earlier builds get their shift-quality stats filled in from stored rows the next time the app opens without a session.

- Every pop-up has a sticky header with a ✕ close button at the top right, so nothing needs scrolling to dismiss.

- Per-vehicle separation: drives are stored with the vehicle id and loaded for the selected vehicle only; screens, rules, custom requests, repairs, usual bands and review history are all keyed to it. A VIN-triggered switch at connect swaps all of them, not just the profile.

- Vehicle summary (Data & sources → Vehicle summary): totals for the selected vehicle (drives, miles, hours, dates, codes seen, repairs) and, for every measure with a band, the median, usual band, all-time range, the rule limit, and a chart of the per-drive values in date order with the band shaded, the median drawn, the limits dashed, and post-repair drives highlighted. Tap a row to open that reading.

- Scan everything (Data & sources): one pass over all eleven DID ranges for the engine (7E0), transmission (7E2), alternate (7E1) and body (241) modules, about 10–15 minutes parked with the engine running; a module silent through its first two ranges is skipped. Results are saved per vehicle (last six scans, with rpm/coolant/voltage at the time) and included in the history export; nothing joins live polling unless added as a reading. A scan with the engine off is accepted but flagged, since pressures and rpm-driven values read zero.

- Watch addresses through a drive (Data & sources): list up to a handful of DIDs for a module and they are polled about once a second on every screen for the rest of the session, logged in the rows as w<header><did> and carried in the export, so a pressure can be told from a temperature by how it moves with rpm. Ends with the session or Stop watching; nothing persists.

- GM requests added from the Equinox scan (OBDb-defined on the LaCrosse/CTS/Camaro/Terrain/Colorado sets, all Unverified): per-cylinder misfire history since codes were cleared (1201–1204, 11F8–11FB) and the total (1200); injector pulse width by bank (12C3/12C4) with a bank-skew finding; ethanol estimate (0052); engine torque (203F); total knock retard (12D9); transmission output speed (1942) and 6T70 C3 clutch slip (19D4). Fuel and Transmission presets carry the new tiles; each enters the usual bands.

- Requests this vehicle answers (Data & sources): the app remembers, per vehicle, which GM requests answered and on which header; the next session starts on that header and parks known-silent requests until the normal 60 s retry. "Probe all known requests" settles the whole set in under a minute on a new vehicle, and "Show list" prints the per-request status.
- Compare the last two full scans (Data & sources): addresses that appeared, vanished or changed between the two most recent saved scans — engine-off against engine-running finds the live values.
- Sierra transmission requests (OBDb Silverado): pressure-control solenoid commanded and actual current (199F/199E) and C1 clutch slip (2851), on the Transmission preset.

- Composite KPIs per drive (in the bands and the Vehicle summary): long-term trim common mode and bank 1 minus bank 2; share of running time at 14.5 V+ and at 13.2 V or less; coolant peak above ambient; oil peak above coolant peak; trans fluid above coolant at cruise; idle time with short-term trim beyond ±8%; share of misfires on the worst cylinder; EVAP tank-pressure range within a drive; warm intake air temperature.
- Vehicle summary now opens with "KPIs and hypotheses" for the selected vehicle: each open question, the KPIs that would confirm or kill it, the recent three-drive median against the frozen band, and a one-word verdict (improving / same / degrading / changed). The lists are per vehicle (Sierra: transmission, cooler, pressure control, AFM misfires, charging; Equinox: bank trim split, trim vs fuel, cylinders 5/6, knock vs intake temperature, charging recovery, coolant peaks, idle lean excursions; Lucerne: P0446 frozen sensor, coolant, bank 1 trim, inferred shift quality, charging).
- Saved drives are re-reviewed in the background once per stats version so older drives carry the newer measures.
- Chassis and body codes (experimental, read-only; Data & sources): asks the brake/stability (EBCM, 243), body (BCM, 241) and cluster (24C) modules, then the ECM and TCM, for their own stored codes with ISO 14229 $19 02 (status mask FF) and, if a module doesn't understand that, GM's $A9 81. C- and B-codes behind a Service StabiliTrak message live here, not in the emissions code read. Both services are reads; the allowlist admits only these two and never a clear. Which service each module answers and the exact answer layout are unverified until seen on the vehicle, so the raw answer is kept beside whatever decodes; ISO answers decode to code, failure-type byte and status bits; GMLAN answers are marked raw/unverified. Results save with the drive and roll into the Maintenance brief under their own system.
- EVAP annunciator: -1,500 Pa held for 60 s. The Sierra sits at -1,000 to -1,400 Pa for 40 s to several minutes on most drives while purging, before and after the transmission repair, with no code in 5,000+ miles, so the earlier -1,000 Pa / 20 s rule chipped on normal purge. The GM -7.5 mmHg figure stays in the reading's note as what the vent-restriction test looks for under its own conditions. The Maintenance brief leaves out alerts logged under the earlier misfire (+1) and EVAP rules.
- Trouble-code definitions (Nov 27 2026): the app carries the SAE J2012 generic set, 2,082 codes (P0, P2, P34xx, P0A-P0C, U0, B0, C0), each shown with its standard definition on the Diagnostics screen, in the review's Codes finding, in the new-code annunciator's spoken line, and in the Maintenance brief. Base list from python-OBD's codes.py with the bank/sensor designations the standard assigns restored for the ranges that define them (cam/crank, HO2S heaters, O2 sensors, fuel trim, knock, cam sensors, catalysts, O2 bias), each range checked against a second published list; samples across every family compared with two further lists with no disagreements. Manufacturer-specific codes (P1xxx, P30xx-P33xx, U1/U2, B1-B3, C1-C3) are deliberately not defined: their meanings differ by make and model year, so the app shows the family and says to look the code up for this vehicle. Tap a code for its definition, what the test means, which app readings bear on it, and the first checks (plain-language notes for the ~55 codes most likely on these three vehicles: misfire, fuel trim, thermostat, EVAP, charging, transmission sensors, ratio, converter clutch, solenoids, slip, module, network, AFM). Freeze frame now reads mode 02 PID 02 to learn which code stored it (nothing is shown when none is stored) and more PIDs (trims both banks, fuel status, MAP, IAT, throttle, voltage), with a one-line account of what the engine was doing when the code set.
- Misfires: the annunciator needs +5 on one cylinder in a drive (amber) or +20 (red) instead of +1, and the review cross-checks the GM per-cylinder counters against the computer's own mode 06 self-test (MIDs A2-A9): the same leading cylinder confirms the mapping; a self-test outside its limit is reported as the computer's verdict. Findings state the rate against the order-of-magnitude code threshold (10-20 per 1,000 revolutions).
- Rough upshifts now split into harsh (completed hard with the converter locked at 30%+ throttle and a 300+ rpm step, or flared: the jolt) and soft (slip spike with the converter released: the slur). A unit can trade one for the other and keep the same rough total; the Sierra hypothesis tracks harsh as the measure that follows the seat. Stats v6 back-fills both.
- Nov 26 2026 fixes: cylinder mode (V4/V8) is computed again — it needs the eight injector-pulse requests, which weren't being polled; they now poll at the slow cadence. Fuel level is the median of the last nine readings (the sender swings 20+ points between samples on a low tank). A request that goes silent twice rests five minutes and is retried instead of being parked for the whole drive (oil temperature was missing for 22 minutes on one drive). Guided health check: unticked steps show their progress (e.g. "Warm idle · 39/120 s"), the firm-acceleration step accepts 50% throttle or 80% load for 2 s (GM's throttle PID tops out near 84% here), and the start-while-connected step tolerates cranking rows between off and running.
- The EVAP annunciator needs tank vacuum beyond -1,000 Pa held for 20 s; momentary purge pulls (3–8 per drive on the Sierra, every drive) no longer chime.
- Pickup build (Nov 24 2026): the Transmission screen carries the cooling-stack tiles (fan command, grille shutter, A/C high-side pressure, ambient) so they are polled at the visible rate; Health check adds trans fluid and fan. New per-drive measure "Trans warm-up, cold to 180°F" (running minutes from a sub-120°F start; the 6L80 thermal bypass valve routes fluid around the cooler until warm, so a valve stuck open warms slowly and one stuck closed warms fast then runs hot); the existing highway heat-build rate is relabelled "Trans fluid heat-build at 65+ mph". Both join the Sierra "Cooling stack" hypothesis.

- Maintenance brief (Data & sources, Drive review, Case file): the answer to "what brings you in today?" for the selected vehicle. Every red/amber alert, trouble code and review finding across the saved drives, rolled up system → class (red alert, trouble code, finding: needs attention, amber alert, finding: watch) → type, with drives affected, share, occurrences, first and last seen, and a trend (new, rising, steady, falling, cleared, once) from the later half of the drives against the earlier half; finding titles that differ only by their numbers are one type. Every banded measure with min / median / max across drives, the usual band, the reading's limit (peaks against the high limit, minimums against the low one; averages and cruise-only rules aren't compared), drives beyond the limit and outside the band, and the recent-three-drive trend. "What brings me in" lists the actions the pattern recommends: a red alert on 2+ drives, any trouble code, an attention finding on a quarter of drives, an amber alert on half, a measure beyond its limit on 2+ drives, a watchlist KPI degrading beyond its band, and adapter dropouts (flagged as not a vehicle fault). "Download brief for the shop" writes it as Markdown. Each saved drive now keeps its review's findings and system statuses (compact), and older drives are re-reviewed once in the background so their findings join the brief.

- Code review (Nov 18 2026): annunciators are judged once a second in the poll loop on every screen, not only while the cockpit is showing; on screens without the cockpit the chips appear in a strip above the MARK bar (tap to acknowledge), so a red condition chimes, speaks and reaches the alert log whatever tab is up. The link-loss alert now counts only answers from the vehicle (derived readings such as trip distance and altitude no longer keep it alive), and a resumed drive's replayed rows no longer trigger a false "no data". Slow readings (fuel, tires, oil life, mode-06 misfire counters, odometer) are logged on the first tick after they answer instead of within a fixed 1.1 s, so a tick delayed by an adapter timeout no longer drops them from the rows. A request that times out shows "--" instead of its last value. The chime is armed and the alert log reset on every way in, including "Reconnect and continue". The oil-pressure route that answered is remembered per vehicle, so the next session starts on it rather than walking the chain again. Estimated MPG uses the same lambda-corrected fuel flow as the trip and 10-minute figures. Text readings (cylinder mode) no longer produce NaN statistics. New bands: cooling fan command (average, peak), grille shutter, A/C high-side peak, ambient average; new Sierra hypothesis "Cooling stack after the radiator and condenser".

### Body-module and extra GM requests (from OBDb recordings on a 2016 Silverado 1500)
- Tire pressures: request header 241, response 641, DIDs 5005–5008, 16-bit kPa ÷ 16. The adapter receive filter (ATCRA) is set for the body module and cleared afterwards. A "Tire pressures" tile is on the Trip page.
- Oil pressure: DID 2345 (×4 kPa) first. A request that answers zero for 10 samples with the engine running is treated as a dead address (the 2014 Equinox answers 2345 with 0): the app then tries 115C (OBDb Equinox/Silverado: raw×0.6−21.6 psi, raw 36 = 0 psi, slope unverified), then 2344 (absolute ×4 kPa minus barometric), then 1C1B (the ECM's calculated oil pressure, a model rather than a sensor, labelled as such), and parks the reading if none responds. A zero while running is never shown or rated on these routes, so a dead address cannot show 0 psi or raise the oil-pressure alert; a real loss of pressure shows as a falling number, not an exact zero. The 2014 Equinox answers 2345, 115C and 2344 with zero. 1470 answers zero on the Sierra. Added: transmission input speed (7E2 1941), cooling fan command (13AF), active grille shutter (2366), ECM ignition voltage (1141), A/C high-side pressure (1564, scaling single-source). Converter slip from input speed is derived as engine rpm minus input speed and can be compared with the reported slip request.
- All of these remain tagged Unverified until they pass the app's physics checks on this truck.

### Equinox and Lucerne parity
- GM cylinder balance rates (7E0 DIDs 162F–1636, recorded on a 2014 Equinox and 2016 Silverado in OBDb) are polled on every vehicle; the review names a cylinder that runs consistently weak at warm idle, checks that the rates roughly cancel before trusting them, and feeds the one-cylinder ignition thesis.
- Requests can carry an alternate route: tire pressures fall back from the body module (241) to the older engine-module DIDs (248E–2491) when the first never answers.
- Vehicles without a gear request get an inferred converter slip (engine rpm minus the speed-implied rpm for the inferred gear).
- The raw-request scan covers more ranges (0000, 0100, 1600, 2300 added) for vehicles like the 2006 Lucerne whose transmission data lives at addresses the newer lists don't cover.

## Tick Finder (`tick-finder.html`)
A separate single-file tool for locating a ticking noise (built for an HVAC tick that starts and stops with the blower) using the phone's microphone. It runs fully offline with no network requests. Nothing is stored or uploaded, apart from a CSV you export yourself.

- Live spectrogram, frequency graph and a 60-second level/tick-score line graph.
- Capture fan OFF and fan ON to see which frequency band the fan adds. Auto mode locks onto the band whose envelope is most periodic and impulsive (the tick score). You can also tap the spectrum to set a custom band.
- Record 5-second samples at labeled spots to rank locations by band level, and at each fan speed to test whether the tick rate follows blower speed (points to the blower wheel) or stays flat (points to an actuator, relay or speed control).
- Tick rate is detected from about 3 to 300 per second. Levels are relative dBFS, not calibrated SPL. It's a locating aid, not a diagnosis.
- The mic needs a secure page, so open it from the GitHub Pages URL rather than a local file on Android.
